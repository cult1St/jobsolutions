# JobSolutions – Connecting Talent with Opportunity

JobSolutions is a modern job board and recruitment platform designed to make hiring and job seeking easy, efficient, and user-friendly.  
This project allows **employers** to post and manage job listings and **job seekers** to browse, filter, and apply for opportunities that match their skills and career goals.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Job Listings:** Post, edit, and manage job opportunities with detailed descriptions.
- **Application Management:** Employers can track applicants; job seekers can apply directly on the platform.
- **Search & Filtering:** Find jobs by title, company, category, or location.
- **Authentication:** Secure login/registration for employers and job seekers.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage users, job posts, and settings with ease.
